How the drum works
Every card has one coordinate on a closed ring path: a pile slot (0 front, 1–2 peeking, 3 hidden), the drop in front of the pile down to the turn (clear below the card it uncovers), and the tuck behind the pile from the turn up to flush behind its lower edge. Each coordinate has its own critically damped spring toward a target that is a pure function of (card, front): a leaver's target is two steps away, so it passes the turn still moving and decelerates into the tuck, and a returning card runs the same ring backwards. The only draw-order change is at the turn, and a card crosses it only when it is clear of what it swaps with. A press is taken once every card in transit has passed its flip, so the pace is whatever the trips allow; tempo evens it out and fold shortens trips while a key is held. An edge budget (24 px/frame, 30 when held) caps all motion. Source: src/drum.mjs.
